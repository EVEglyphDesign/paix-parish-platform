Sunday — Mass in English10:00 AM

The Sunday Mass at 10:00 AM in English is confirmed by the PVAngels worship directory, by the Villa Encantada visitor pages, and by repeated first-hand witness reports, but no diocesan source publishes it. There is no parish office and no published telephone number. A separate interdenominational Christian service meets at 10:30 AM in the Salón del Sol of that tourist site — which is not this Mass.
