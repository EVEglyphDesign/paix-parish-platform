Our parish

The "Frankish church" of the Old Town — the oldest monument to the Philhellenes in Greece.

Founded in 1840, Ιερός Καθολικός Ναός Μεταμορφώσεως του Σωτήρος serves the community of Ναύπλιο, Αργολίδα. It is part of Sacred Catholic Archdiocese of Athens.

A monument under preservation — within the declared historic site of the Old Town of Nafplio.

This page presents the parish. For the full history, biographies of the priests and the history of the church, visit the parish's official site: cathecclesia.gr
