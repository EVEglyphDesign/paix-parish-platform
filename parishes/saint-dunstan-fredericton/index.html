Welcome

Welcome to the parish website.

St. Dunstan's Church, founded in 1827, is located in Fredericton, New Brunswick. This site is a non-official mirror prepared under the EVE Glyph Design doctrine, with the same editorial care as the parish's official site. The official source remains stmarymagdaleneparish.ca.

One parish, one people

Faith lived locally, with a community that prays, celebrates and cares for one another.
