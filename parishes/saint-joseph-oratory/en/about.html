Our parish

Minor basilica and national shrine — founded in 1904 by saint brother André.

Founded in 1904, Oratoire Saint-Joseph du Mont-Royal serves the community of Montréal, Québec. It is part of Archdiocese of Montréal · Congregation of Holy Cross.

National shrine and minor basilica — not a territorial parish.

This page presents the parish. For the full history, biographies of the priests and the history of the church, visit the parish's official site: saint-joseph.org
